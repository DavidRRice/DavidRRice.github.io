Background

My research brings together interests that have been with me since my undergraduate studies: astronomy, physics, and the Earth and planetary sciences. I now use that combination to investigate the compositions and architectures of planets beyond the Solar System.

Although I love a big city, home still means Iowa for me. I grew up in a small suburb of the suburbs of Des Moines. I enjoy getting outdoors through travelling, camping, hiking, and, of course, stargazing.

Service to the community

I co-coordinate PEARL, contribute to the PLATO small-planet working group, and served on the scientific organizing committee for Layers of Understanding: Model Intercomparisons of Exoplanet Interiors in Heidelberg.

In addition, I am a frequent journal refereee and serve on the UW–Madison Astronomy Culture Committee.
